Version 3.16

Secondlight 3.16 adds control over flash metadata when exporting photos and improves account setup reliability.

• Choose Keep Original, Fired, or Did Not Fire in the Export panel's Flash Metadata setting.
• Apply the selected flash status to JPEG, PNG, TIFF, layered PSD, batch exports, and Photoshop handoff while preserving other photo metadata.
• Fixed an account setup issue that could prevent new users from loading their free credits or opening credit checkout.
