Version 3.20

Layer review is faster in Secondlight 3.20.

- Use Up and Down to select and show one layer at a time, following the visible layer list.
- Apply color labels with A (red), S (orange), D (yellow), F (green), G (blue), and H (purple).
- Assign one, two, or three stars with 1, 2, and 3.
- Press / or ? to toggle label filtering and restore the previous filter and refinement settings.
- Clone Stamp now uses Shift+S.
- Layer edits support Undo and Redo, and shortcuts leave text entry unchanged.
