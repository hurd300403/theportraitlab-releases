Version 3.19.2

Secondlight 3.19.2 improves editor stability and adds more ways to organize and create alternatives.

- Fixes a freeze when filtering color-labeled layers, including the Green filter.
- Fixes canvas slowdowns and freezes with large brushes.
- Adds layer tiers, tier filtering and sorting, and larger thumbnails in the Layers panel.
- Adds tab-scoped batch controls, confirmation before clearing a tab, and recovery of cleared rows.
- Improves saved style mix protection with recoverable deletion and more reliable batch copying.
