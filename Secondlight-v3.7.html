Version 3.7

Secondlight 3.7 introduces a major upgrade to hands-on retouching and canvas reliability.

- Adds high-fidelity Brush, Mask, Eraser, Clone Stamp, and Liquify workflows with live Diameter, Hardness, Opacity, Flow, pressure, mesh, pin-edge, and review controls.
- Makes brush spacing, mid-stroke adjustments, undo, cancel, save, and export behavior deterministic and reliable.
- Improves mask preview modes, clipping groups, blend modes, transformed edges, and CPU/GPU compositing parity.
- Preserves detail in untouched Liquify regions and improves performance and memory behavior on large documents.
- Strengthens canvas, generation, Photoshop sync, session, and export workflows.
- Remembers the most recently used layer color in the radial menu.
